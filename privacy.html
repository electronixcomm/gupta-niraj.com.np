Privacy & terms

Short and plain: this site does not track you.

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no forms that send data anywhere.

Services the pages rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web server it receives your IP address when you load a page; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser contacts Google's font servers; see the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Some lessons show diagrams from Wikimedia Commons or load a diagram library from jsDelivr.

Saved progress

Some lessons remember quiz answers, ticked lab tasks or your light/dark choice in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Using the teaching material

All lessons are free to read and study. You may quote short passages with a link to the lesson page. For any other reuse, such as copying whole lessons, printing for a course pack or translating, please ask first. © Niraj Kumar Gupta, all rights reserved.

Third-party images keep their own licences, credited where they appear.
