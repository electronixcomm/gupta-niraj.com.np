A GAN is a deep-learning framework with two networks trained together: a generator that synthesises fake samples from random noise, and a discriminator that tries to distinguish fakes from real data. They play a minimax game — the generator improves until the discriminator can no longer reliably tell real from fake. GANs are the foundation of modern image synthesis, including the face-ageing model used in my Master's thesis.

Supervised learning trains on labelled input-output pairs (e.g., images with class labels). Unsupervised learning finds structure in unlabelled data (e.g., clustering, dimensionality reduction). Reinforcement learning trains an agent through trial and error in an environment, optimising cumulative reward over time.
