Waveforms AC/DC Foundations
Q1.The peak value of a sinusoidal AC waveform is also called its:
  • RMS value
  • Average value
  • Maximum amplitude
  • Time period
Explanation: The peak value is the maximum instantaneous value reached by the waveform from the zero reference — the maximum amplitude.

Topics queued: RMS vs. average, frequency & period, phase difference, power factor.

The RMS (root-mean-square) value is the equivalent DC value that would deliver the same power to a resistor — for a pure sine wave, RMS = peak ÷ √2 ≈ 0.707 × peak. The average value over a half-cycle is 2/π × peak ≈ 0.637 × peak. RMS is used for power calculations; average is used for instruments like moving-coil meters with rectifiers.

Period (T) is the time taken for one complete cycle, measured in seconds. Frequency (f) is the number of cycles per second, measured in hertz: f = 1/T. Angular frequency (ω) is the rate of change of phase angle in radians per second: ω = 2πf.
