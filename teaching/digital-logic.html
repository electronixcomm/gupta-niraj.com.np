Digital Logic

Course material for BT102CO — number systems, Boolean algebra, simplification, combinational and sequential logic, registers and counters. Each unit is an interactive study page with worked examples and practice problems.

BT102CO Purvanchal University Gateway Professional College
1

Unit 1 · Number Systems

Binary, octal, decimal and hexadecimal; conversions, complements and binary codes.
2

Unit 2 · Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates

Boolean theorems and functions, logic gates, canonical forms and logic families.
3

Unit 3 · Simplification of Boolean Functions

Minterms and maxterms, 2–4 variable K-maps, prime implicants, SOP and POS.
4

Unit 4 · Combinational Logic

Adders and subtractors, code conversion, multilevel NAND/NOR circuits, XOR and parity.
5

Unit 5 · Combinational Logic with MSI & LSI

Parallel and BCD adders, comparators, decoders, encoders, multiplexers, ROM and PLA.
6

Unit 6 · Sequential Logic

Flip-flops (RS, D, JK, T, master-slave), state equations and state reduction.
7

Unit 7 · Registers & Counters

Shift registers, SISO/SIPO/PISO/PIPO, synchronous and asynchronous counters, ring and decade counters.
